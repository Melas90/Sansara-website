## FAQ

eyebrow: Good questions
title: Things people *ask before a project*.

### items

- q: How much does a project cost?
  a: One fixed price per project, agreed in writing before we start, and it doesn't change halfway. We give you the figure on the intro call, once we know the scope.
- q: How long does it take?
  a: It depends on the piece and on what you already have. What we promise is a date, agreed on the intro call and put in writing, and plain updates while we build.
- q: I already have a website and a logo. Do I start from zero?
  a: No. We look at what you have, keep what works, and build on top of it. From scratch only when there's nothing worth keeping.
- q: Who owns what you build?
  a: You do. Accounts, domains, tools and files are in your name from day one, and you get a walkthrough at handover.
- q: Can I combine several areas?
  a: Yes. Each is a project on its own, and together they become the Client Loop. If you want the whole thing built and run as one, look at The System.
- q: Do you also run the ads or post the content?
  a: Running ads is part of The System. Posting is part of the Complete tier of Content and Social Media. Each page says exactly what's included.
