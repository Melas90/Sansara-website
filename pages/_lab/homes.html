Five directions for the home page

Same words, same colours, five different characters. They move step by step away from the brief: the first keeps everything the brief specifies and only recomposes it; the fifth overrules most of it. Open each one, scroll it on a phone too, and say which one feels like Sansara. Mixing is allowed.

  1. 1. Ledger Closest to the brief. Pills, the italic serif word, the warm band all stay. The change is composition: no cards anywhere, the three offers are wide rows divided by hairlines like a printed price list, and the type gets much bigger. Quiet, expensive, unhurried.
  2. 2. Grid Swiss rationality. One typeface only, square corners, a visible twelve-column grid with a small running label in the left margin of every section, ember used once as a thick rule and once as the button. Overrules the brief on the serif word and the pills. Clear, professional, no decoration.
  3. 3. Sun The seventies warmth of the wordmark taken seriously. Headlines in the serif, light and large; an ember disc rises behind the hero once; the statement sits on a full ember band; the offers stand in arch-topped panels. Overrules the brief on the type roles. The most "brand" of the five.
  4. 4. Night Dark first. Espresso ground, cream type, ember light; the warm gradient becomes the one bright island for the testimonial. Pills and the serif word stay. Overrules the brief on the ground colour. Premium, calm, very different from every cream agency site.
  5. 5. Poster The furthest away. Everything square, no pills, no serif, no cards, no entrance animations. The headline runs edge to edge at poster size, sections are full-bleed blocks of cream, ember and ink, calls to action are underlined words, and a slow marquee carries the four service areas. Loud and confident.
