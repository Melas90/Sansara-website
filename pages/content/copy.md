## FAQ

eyebrow: Good questions
title: Things you *might be wondering*.

### items

- q: Do I need to know anything about editing?
  a: No. You record on your phone and drop the files in a folder. We plan, write, edit and design. You never open an editing app.
- q: Will it look good?
  a: Yes. During setup we build a design system in your colors, fonts and style, so everything looks like you. But looking good is where we start, not where we stop. Every piece also has a job.
- q: What's the difference between Starter and Complete?
  a: On Starter, you speak in your own words, we turn it into finished content, and you post it. On Complete, we also write your scripts and the strategy, publish for you, and prepare the best pieces to run as ads.
- q: Do you promise followers or sales?
  a: No, and be careful with anyone who does. We promise a strategy behind every piece, a clear next step to your offer, and an honest monthly look at which pieces brought enquiries.
- q: Are ads included?
  a: The content is built to work as ads, and on Complete we flag the pieces that are ready. Running the ads is part of The System. Your ad budget is always paid by you, in your own account.
- q: What if I'm late with my clips?
  a: What's in your folder on the 5th is what we make that month. If you send less, you get less that month; the plan for the next month stays the same.
- q: Can I change things after I've approved the text?
  a: Small fixes, yes. Every finished piece has one round of changes. Rewriting approved text counts as a new piece.
- q: Do you use AI?
  a: For layout and first drafts, always checked by a person. Your real voice, always. We never use an AI voice or your likeness without your written consent.
- q: Who owns the content?
  a: You do. Your footage and every finished piece are yours to use as you like.
- q: How long do I commit for?
  a: Three months to start, because content needs a few months to show what works. Then month to month.
