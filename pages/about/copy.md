## Story

### sections

- title: What ten years *taught us*
  body:
  - Single pieces rarely change a business. A nice landing page here, one clever campaign there. It helps, then it fades, because everything around it is still held together by hand.
  - "We saw it again and again: owners doing everything they were told to, posting, replying fast, following up, and still chasing. Not because they weren't working hard enough. Because all of it ran through them."
  - So we stopped selling pieces as if they were answers, and started building the whole path, connected, and handing it over working.
- title: Why we stay *small*
  body:
  - When you work with us, you work with us. There is no account manager between you and the people doing the work, and nobody learning on your budget.
  - Small also means we take on only a few businesses at a time, and only when we think it will work. If it won't, we'll tell you on the first call.
- title: Where the name *comes from*
  body:
  - "Sansara is the cycle: things coming round again. It's what we build. Not a one-off burst of attention, but a loop that keeps bringing the right people in, keeps in touch with them, and brings the good ones back."
