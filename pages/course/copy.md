## FAQ

eyebrow: Good questions
title: Things you *might be wondering*.
items:
- q: Kajabi, LearnWorlds or something else?
  a: Kajabi bundles pages, emails and payments in one place; LearnWorlds is stronger on the learning experience itself, with interactive video and assessments. Other platforms on demand. We tell you honestly which fits your course on the intro call.
- q: How long does it take?
  a: It depends on how much of your course is ready. We agree the date on the intro call and put it in writing.
- q: What do I need to bring?
  a: Your course content (videos, lessons, materials), your offer and your price. We handle the rest, and we'll tell you exactly what's missing after the intro call.
- q: Do I need technical skills to run it afterwards?
  a: No. You get a walkthrough at handover, and everything is built so you can edit pages, add lessons and see your numbers yourself.
- q: Who owns the platform and the content?
  a: You do. The account, the domain, the list and the content are in your name from day one, and they stay yours if we stop working together.
