# Design ideas

A running list of things that could make the site better, to bring up one to three at a time at natural moments (brief §0.2). Each is a proposal, not a plan. Status: idea · proposed · chosen · rejected.

| # | Idea | Where | What the visitor feels | Route | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Use the 70s wordmark's letterforms as a large, cropped background shape on the dark footer or a band, ember on ink | Footer, or one band | The brand's own personality, not a generic dark footer | Asset (SVG of the wordmark) | idea |
| 2 | A "you're inside one right now" moment on the System page where the page's own contact and booking flow lights up as the visitor scrolls, instead of a separate diagram | /system | The proof is the page itself | Custom JS | idea |
| 3 | The statement section as one poster-size sentence on the warm gradient, with the italic word rising a beat after the rest | Home, statement | A pause between hero and offers | Custom CSS | idea |
| 4 | Consulting path (Kickstarter → Growth → Scale) as three stations on one line, the line drawn as you scroll, current station filled | /consulting | Progress, not a price list | Custom JS (Round 5) | idea |
| 5 | Services cards that show the four areas as short lists, not icons; hovering one lifts nothing but shifts the name into italic serif | /services, Home | Type does the work | Custom CSS (Round 4 "font shift") | idea |
| 6 | Testimonial band on the dark gradient with the quote in Newsreader italic at H2 size and a small round portrait, once consent exists | Home, proof | One voice, trusted | Custom CSS | idea |
| 7 | An SVG version of the wordmark for sharper rendering and a favicon cut from its "S" | Header, tab icon | Crisp on every screen | Asset (owner's brand folder) | proposed (pending marker in head.html) |
| 8 | FAQ as a single column of plain `details` rows with a hairline between, no accordion widget | Home, offer pages | Reads like a conversation | Native | idea |
