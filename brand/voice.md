# Voice

From the brief §3.4, with the tone of the live landing page (`references/live-page-copy.txt`) as the ear.

- **English.** "We" for the studio, "you" for the visitor. American spelling until decision #14 says otherwise; keep it consistent.
- **Warm, direct, plain.** Like an experienced partner across the table. Short sentences. Verbs over nouns.
- **Outcomes over features.** Say what changes for the visitor, then how.
- **No hype.** Never "revolutionary", "explode", "guaranteed", "unlock your potential". No exclamation marks in headlines.
- **No jargon without a one-sentence explanation.** Explain "funnel" once, then use it.
- **Honest.** We promise that things are built, connected, tested, working and in the client's name. Never a number of bookings, leads or revenue.
- **Sentence case** for headings and buttons. Buttons say what happens: "Book a free call", "Send message", "Explore Consulting".
- **The emotional half of a headline** is one or two words in italic serif, marked `*like this*` in copy.md. One per headline at most, and not in every headline.
- **Missing facts are marked, never invented.** `PENDING: what is needed` in copy.md.

Things to avoid on the page because they read as generated: strings of items joined with middle dots, "WORD — fragment" labels, numbered lists that are not sequences, tracked-caps labels above every heading, an arrow on every link.
