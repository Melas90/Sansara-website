# Positioning

From the brief §1. The words here are the source for copy; the copy itself lives in `pages/*/copy.md`.

**Sansara Media** is a small digital studio. *We are the expert partner who takes digital marketing off your plate, so you can focus on your core business.*

**Audience:** small businesses and solopreneurs who are good at their core work and don't want to spend their time on marketing. Which markets first: open decision #13.

**Three offers, separated by who takes responsibility** (the manufacturer analogy):

| Offer | Analogy | Client brings | We own |
|---|---|---|---|
| The System (flagship; final name is decision #1) | Design and build, turnkey | A problem: "I need clients to book" | The whole solution, end to end: ad → page → follow-up → booking, until it runs |
| Consulting | The consulting engineer | Their own team and time | The direction. Programs: Kickstarter → Growth → Scale |
| Services (done for you) | Build to print | A clear brief: "I need a website" | Executing it well. Areas: web design · branding and graphic design · funnel and email marketing · e-learning and online courses |

**The homepage is the brand's front door**, not a sales page for one offer. The three offers appear side by side, as equals. Deep selling (leaks, flow diagram, pricing, case study, fit lists) happens on each offer's own page.

**Helper for undecided visitors:** do you know what you need? Exactly → Services. Just want it to work → The System. Want to do it yourself with an expert beside you → Consulting.

**Promise boundary:** built, connected, tested, working, in the client's name. Never bookings, leads or revenue figures.

**Not on the website:** digital products (courses, templates); they are sold through email funnels only.

**Main goal of every page:** `/book-a-call`. The contact form on the home page is the lighter alternative.

**Kickstarter / Growth / Scale** are used only in the Consulting context.
